WHERE LEADS LEAK

Fix the handoffs that leave consultation enquiries waiting.

The objective is not generic automation. It is to make specific handoffs around missed calls, after-hours enquiries, approved routing, human consultation booking and follow-up easier to handle consistently.

Missed-call recovery

Give prospective clients a clear next path after a missed call instead of leaving the enquiry in voicemail or an unowned inbox.

After-hours intake

Capture caller-provided contact and enquiry details outside normal coverage and route them into the approved workflow.

Consultation-intent capture

Pass caller-provided service or consultation intent to the responsible staff member without repeated manual copying.

Approved staff routing

Move an enquiry to the appropriate staff member, queue or established escalation path with a visible next owner.

Human callback and booking handoffs

Connect the agreed next step to a human callback or consultation-booking process with clear human ownership.

Follow-up and CRM sync

Keep lead records, statuses and useful context aligned across the tools your team already uses.

MED SPA LEAD RECOVERY FAQ

What this engagement actually means.

Does this provide medical advice or determine suitability?

No. Licensed clinicians and qualified staff remain responsible for diagnosis, treatment recommendations, eligibility, suitability and final clinical decisions.

Can this handle sensitive or urgent questions?

It can route them to the responsible human team and its established escalation path. It does not provide emergency or clinical advice or replace professional judgement.

Can this handle after-hours med spa enquiries?

Those are common starting points. The appropriate acknowledgement, intake, routing and escalation rules depend on the systems, coverage and operating handoffs your team already uses.

Is this a generic chatbot?

No. Conversational tools can be one component, but the core work is the lead path: intake, context, routing, human callback or consultation-booking handoff, follow-up state and operational visibility.
