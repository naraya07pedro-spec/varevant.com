WHERE LEADS LEAK

Fix the handoffs that leave estimate enquiries waiting.

The objective is not generic automation. It is to make specific handoffs around missed calls, after-hours enquiries, estimate requests, human estimate-visit booking and follow-up easier to handle consistently.

Missed-call recovery

Give prospective customers a clear next path after a missed call instead of leaving the enquiry in voicemail or an unowned inbox.

After-hours intake

Capture caller-provided contact and enquiry details outside normal coverage and route them into the approved workflow.

Project-detail capture

Pass caller-provided project, service, location and timing details to the responsible team without repeated manual copying.

Estimate-request routing

Move an estimate request to the appropriate team member, queue or established escalation path with a visible next owner.

Human callback and visit handoffs

Connect the agreed next step to a human callback or estimate-visit booking process with clear human ownership.

Estimate follow-up and CRM sync

Keep lead records, statuses and useful context aligned across the tools your team already uses.

LANDSCAPING LEAD RECOVERY FAQ

What this engagement actually means.

Does this determine the project scope or price?

No. Your team remains responsible for site assessment, job scope, pricing and final service decisions. The system can capture caller-provided context and route it to the right human owner.

Can this handle after-hours landscaping enquiries?

Those are common starting points. The appropriate acknowledgement, intake, routing and escalation rules depend on the systems, coverage and operating handoffs your team already uses.

Does this provide horticultural or site-safety advice?

No. It does not replace qualified human judgement about plants, site conditions, safety or the work to be performed.

Is this a generic chatbot?

No. Conversational tools can be one component, but the core work is the lead path: intake, context, routing, human callback or estimate-visit handoff, follow-up state and operational visibility.
