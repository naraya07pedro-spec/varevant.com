What is slow lead response potentially costing you?

Use your own operating numbers to estimate the commercial exposure created by missed or delayed leads. This tool does not use hidden industry averages, invented benchmarks, or guaranteed outcomes.

Evidence rule: every result below is calculated only from the values you enter. Treat it as a planning estimate, then validate it against your CRM, call logs, completed jobs, and gross profit records.

This is not a promise of recoverable revenue. Capacity, lead quality, seasonality, sales execution, service area, pricing, and attribution can materially change actual outcomes.

How to make the estimate useful.

1. Use observed lead counts

Pull a real period from your phone system, web forms, CRM, or inbox. Remove irrelevant contacts before calculating.

2. Use your own conversion data

Do not borrow a generic close-rate benchmark. Compare reached leads with completed jobs or customers in your own records.

3. Validate after automation

Track the original lead through response, appointment, sale, and gross profit. Measure recovered outcomes rather than assuming them.
